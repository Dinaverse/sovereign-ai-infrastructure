## ⚠️ Privacy & Security Checklist (READ BEFORE UPLOADING)
Before uploading any screenshots or photos of your lab, ensure the following to protect your sovereignty:
1.  **Blur/Obfuscate:**
    - Any visible IP addresses (e.g., in terminal prompts or network settings).
    - Email addresses, usernames, or real names.
    - Serial numbers on physical hardware.
    - Background clutter (e.g., personal items, calendars).
2.  **Metadata:** Use tools like `exiftool` to strip EXIF data from images (location, device info).
3.  **Content:** Prefer architectural diagrams or configuration dashboards over raw photos of screens where possible.
